# Combat balance baseline — 14 October 2026

Each cell: **win % ± batch SD · median damage taken / median finishing HP**.
Both HP medians include every fight; losses finish at 0 HP. Damage is cumulative HP actually lost to hits, before healing, and can exceed starting health.
Each cell uses ten independent batches of twenty fights (200 total). SD measures variation in batch win rates, in percentage points.

Start: full HP, zero rage/combo points, no prebuffs or consumables, no kill XP/level-up heals. Public game actions, normal swing/ability timing, all enemies initially adjacent and hostile. Janos free Warrior outfit. Rogue and all-class builds: DW = Battleworn Hammer (3–5, 2.9 turns) + Dirk (1–3, 1.6 turns); 2H = Short Staff (5–8, 3.2 turns). Janos sells the Dirk for 57 copper and the Short Staff for 102 copper. Both remove the shield and preserve the other armor. Starting HP: L1 51, L2 57, L3 72, L5 102.

Garrick uses the live L5 profile. Level-3 groups use the laborer profile, level-4 groups use the thug profile, and level-5 groups use the Garrick profile. Historical stalker control: L20, 494 HP, 888 armor. Adopted stalker: L8, 280 HP, 240 armor, 12–16 base damage, 2.5-turn swings. Both stalker rows use player L5.
